# SkillSetu AI
### Bridging Informal Skills to Formal Recognition

SkillSetu AI is an AI-assisted skill assessment platform designed to help individuals with informally acquired skills demonstrate their competencies, identify skill gaps, and connect with learning opportunities, skill recognition pathways, and employment opportunities.

Many people acquire valuable skills through practical experience, apprenticeships, family occupations, and self-learning rather than formal education. However, their abilities may remain undocumented or unrecognized.

SkillSetu AI aims to bridge this gap by providing an accessible digital platform for skill discovery, assessment, evidence submission, and progression toward formal recognition.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Millions of skilled individuals acquire practical knowledge outside traditional educational systems. Despite having valuable industry-relevant abilities, they may lack formal credentials to demonstrate their competencies.

Existing skill recognition pathways can be difficult to navigate, and workers may not know which skills they possess, what they need to improve, or how to access suitable training and employment opportunities.

**Our goal is to make skill assessment and recognition more accessible, understandable, and inclusive.**

---

## 💡 Our Solution

SkillSetu AI provides a structured journey from informal experience to demonstrable competencies.

The platform helps workers:

- Create a profile describing their practical experience and skills.
- Discover relevant skills based on their trade and experience.
- Attempt trade-specific preliminary assessments.
- Identify strengths and areas for improvement.
- Explore courses suited to their skill level.
- Submit practical evidence of their abilities.
- Build a Skill Passport documenting their progress and verified competencies.
- Discover opportunities aligned with their assessed skills.

The platform is designed with accessibility and practical skill demonstration in mind.

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. 👤 Worker Onboarding
Create a personalized profile containing trade, experience, location, and information about how skills were acquired.

### 2. 🧠 Trade-Specific Assessment
Attempt preliminary assessments tailored to different occupations, including:

- Electrician
- Plumber
- Carpenter
- Mechanic
- Mason
- Welder
- Tailor
- Painter

Assessment availability depends on the implemented prototype.

### 3. 📊 Skill Gap Analysis
Understand assessment performance, identify areas for improvement, and explore recommended learning pathways.

### 4. 📚 Personalized Learning Pathways
Explore courses at different levels:

- Foundation
- Intermediate
- Advanced

Course recommendations help workers identify potential next steps in their learning journey.

### 5. 🛠️ Practical Evidence Submission
Support the demonstration of practical abilities through evidence such as photographs or videos, where implemented.

### 6. 🪪 Digital Skill Passport
Maintain a structured record of declared skills, assessment progress, submitted evidence, and assessor-verified competencies.

### 7. 🧑‍🏫 Assessor Dashboard
Provide a dedicated interface for assessors to review submitted evidence and evaluate competencies against assessment criteria.

### 8. 💼 Opportunity Discovery
Help workers explore relevant work opportunities based on their trade and demonstrated competencies.

### 9. 🌐 Accessible User Experience
Use a simple, intuitive interface designed to make the skill recognition journey easier to understand.

## 🔄 How It Works

1. **Register:** Create a worker profile.
2. **Describe:** Provide information about your trade and practical experience.
3. **Assess:** Complete a preliminary skill assessment.
4. **Discover:** Review strengths and potential skill gaps.
5. **Improve:** Explore relevant learning pathways.
6. **Demonstrate:** Submit practical evidence of your abilities.
7. **Verify:** Have submitted evidence reviewed by an authorized assessor.
8. **Build:** Maintain your Skill Passport as your competencies are documented.
9. **Connect:** Explore suitable opportunities.

> Note: Preliminary platform assessments and Skill Passport records do not automatically constitute a government-recognized qualification. Formal recognition or certification depends on the applicable scheme and authorized assessment process.
